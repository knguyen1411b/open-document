# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Focus on technical issues, not individuals
- Accept feedback gracefully

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Repeated disruptive behavior

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all repository spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this policy.
